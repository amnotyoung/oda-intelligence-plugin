# KOICA regulation research protocol

For consequential personnel, disciplinary, financial, procurement, audit, or program-governance questions:

1. Search broadly by the user's issue.
2. Use the public gateway to identify the likely regulation and article.
3. Open the current official source outside the gateway before relying on complete article text.
4. Check provisos, definitions, delegation clauses, effective dates, transitional provisions, and referenced annexes or forms in that official source.
5. Follow cross-regulation references when authority or procedure depends on another rule.
6. Separate the indexed KOICA rule from external statutes or factual applicability.
7. Verify the final citation-bearing text.

Use this answer shape:

- Direct answer
- Governing provisions
- Procedure, criteria, or approval authority
- Exceptions and referenced annexes
- Facts still needed to determine applicability
- Verified citations

If the complete official source was not checked, say so explicitly and narrow the answer. If the indexed text is incomplete or contradictory, state that the result requires confirmation from the current official regulation source or responsible KOICA unit.

## Source attribution

Every gateway tool declares its domain in its description as `[Source: ...]`. The regulation tools — `search_regulation`, `find_references`, `list_sources`, and `verify_citation` — all carry `[Source: koica-regulations]`, an index of KOICA internal regulation text.

That index has **no registered public address**. The gateway is its access path. Say so plainly when a reader asks where the text can be seen, and do not offer a public portal URL that resembles it: KOICA's website, the national law portal, and the public data portal each publish something else, and sending a reader there to confirm an internal rule wastes their time and can substitute the wrong instrument.

Attribute what the index returned instead:

- the regulation title and revision label as `list_sources` gives them, since a citation is only checkable against a stated revision;
- the article numbers the answer rests on;
- whether the complete official text was confirmed outside the gateway, and by what means.

That combination lets a reader take the citation to the responsible KOICA unit or the current official regulation source and check it, which is what an address would have been for.
