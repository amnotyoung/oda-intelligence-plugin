# KOICA regulation research protocol

For consequential personnel, disciplinary, financial, procurement, audit, or program-governance questions:

1. Search broadly by the user's issue.
2. Use the public gateway to identify the likely regulation and article.
3. Open the current official source outside the gateway before relying on complete article text.
4. Check provisos, definitions, delegation clauses, effective dates, transitional provisions, and referenced annexes or forms in that official source.
5. Follow cross-regulation references when authority or procedure depends on another rule.
6. Separate the indexed KOICA rule from external statutes or factual applicability.
7. Verify the final citation-bearing text.

Use this answer shape:

- Direct answer
- Governing provisions
- Procedure, criteria, or approval authority
- Exceptions and referenced annexes
- Facts still needed to determine applicability
- Verified citations

If the complete official source was not checked, say so explicitly and narrow the answer. If the indexed text is incomplete or contradictory, state that the result requires confirmation from the current official regulation source or responsible KOICA unit.
