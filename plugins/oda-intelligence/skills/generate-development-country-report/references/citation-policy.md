# Citation policy

- Cite the public original URL nearest to the claim it supports.
- For development-cooperation documents, include the exact title and original URL when available.
- Do not expose corpus IDs, article IDs, search-result IDs, cache keys, or local snapshot paths to readers.
- Identify the OECD dataset and measure; do not relabel DAC2A observations as CRS.
- Describe IATI as publisher-reported activity data and state relevant completeness or duplication caveats.
- Describe ODA Map status as period-derived unless an official operational status is separately verified.
- Cite KOICA rules as `규정명 제N조`; cite annexes by regulation name and annex label.
- Run `verify_citation` against the final KOICA regulation citations.
- Never cite a source that was not opened, fetched, or returned by a tool during the task.
